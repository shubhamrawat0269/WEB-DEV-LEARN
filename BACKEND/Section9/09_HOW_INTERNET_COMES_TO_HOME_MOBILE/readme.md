1. When we setup wifi in our how, internet fiber cables comes to our home through ISP dedicated workspace center in which a router is also setup which is also connected to physical wire of ocean cables.
2. Wifi max range could be 1 or 2 floor i.e. (40-50 m) range
3. But, When talking about 3G,4G,5G connection towers comes into picture.
4. Towers from any service provider for e.g. Jio, Airtel ranges from 10km - 15km.
5. Just like our home has router which catches internet cable, Tower also connected with internet cable
6. Towers has a dedicated workspace center where the tower are manage properly.
7. Our Cell Phone has a dedicated antena and modem i.e. (antena) => catches the signal; (modem) => electromagnetic signal to digital
signal.
8. When our cell phone get's connected with the tower, tower assigns an IP address to it.
9. Workspace station of a tower responsibilities is to check : 
  A. Whether recharge is there in the cell phone connected to it's tower.
  B. How much balance is there.
  C. Whether to allow user to use network services or not.
  D. Assign IP Address.
